USDC settlement constraints

◎
USDC is not automatically staked

The 2% is modeled as an incentive funded by the selected payer, not a guaranteed yield on escrowed balances.

◎
Custody vs orchestration

Approved custodians/payment providers hold and transmit funds; GFT provides ledger, reconciliation and authorized instructions.

◎
Financing is daily accrual

APR × outstanding principal × days / 365. Early inventory paydown can reduce actual charges.

◎
Unsold inventory remains exposure

POS redemption must not serve as the sole source of principal collection.

How to interpret the model

High capture rates raise GFT revenue but also increase the brand's required reward and processing budget. High store counts raise throughput and aggregate purchasing volume. Longer inventory cover increases financed capital and interest exposure.

Assumption provenance

GFT's $0.75 fee is an input provided for the proposed business model. The 2% USDC incentive and 8–10% APR target are hypothetical. No real-time sales, Corvid pricing or custody balances are connected.
